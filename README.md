# Laredo Border Trade Analysis

I'm studying crossing activity at the Laredo border to see what the
data shows about trade patterns over time.

## Question
How have crossings at Laredo changed over the past several years,
and what might explain the patterns?

## Data
Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) Border Crossing Entry Data
(inbound crossings only).

## Method (planned)
1. Filter to Laredo and chart monthly crossings by type.
2. Identify a pattern, such as seasonality or a dip.
3. Test one possible explanation.

## Limits
